```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Quality Rules

1. **Strict Quality Gates**:
   Verification of all 5 pillars required before closing any milestone.
2. **Zero Technical Debt Guarantee**:
   Ensures long-term stability and maintainability.
